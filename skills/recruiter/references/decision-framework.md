# Decision Framework

Use this framework for a fast, source-grounded GO, NO-GO, or NEEDS VERIFICATION decision. A client-specific intake or current job description always overrides generic examples. Never store a live client target list or intake detail in this package.

## Fast-fit test

A candidate is a strong fit when all four checks are clear:

1. **Environment match:** similar industry, plant type, operating complexity, equipment context, and constraints.
2. **Scope match:** performed the core work, not only adjacent tasks or observation.
3. **Ownership proof:** can explain decisions made, problems owned, people led, and outcomes delivered.
4. **Logistics alignment:** location, shift, travel, work status, availability, and compensation align with verified requirements.

## Confidence

- **High:** direct evidence supports all four checks.
- **Medium:** core work is supported but one material item needs confirmation.
- **Low:** environment mismatch, ownership unclear, or a major requirement is unsupported.

## Decision

- **GO:** all hard gates pass and strongest requirements have direct evidence.
- **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** plausible fit with named questions that can change the decision.
- **NO-GO:** a verified hard gate fails or the candidate lacks the core scope.

Unknown never means positive. Do not convert assumptions, title similarity, employer reputation, or remembered client preferences into evidence.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Candidate resume and explicit call statements.
2. Current job description and approved intake notes.
3. Verified public sources for employer or equipment context.
4. General industry knowledge, used only to form questions.

## Screening order

1. Confirm non-negotiable credentials and legal/work-status requirements.
2. Confirm recent hands-on exposure to the core work.
3. Confirm scope, team size, decision authority, systems, equipment, and measurable outcomes.
4. Check location, commute, shift, travel, compensation, notice, and interview timing.
5. Check job-change pattern using context, not stereotypes.
6. State strongest proof, missing proof, and final decision.

## Transferability

Do not score transferability from company name alone. Compare:

- process type and production volume;
- automation, controls, tooling, and maintenance environment;
- safety and quality systems;
- union/non-union context when relevant;
- leadership level and actual span of control;
- pace, shift model, and operating consequences.

Classify transferability as **Direct**, **Adjacent**, or **Unproven**, with one sentence of evidence.

## Required output

```text
Decision: GO | NEEDS VERIFICATION | NO-GO
Confidence: High | Medium | Low
Strongest proof:
- ...
Material gaps:
- ...
Questions that can change the decision:
- ...
```

No named client, candidate, source-company list, compensation record, or live job detail belongs in this reference.
