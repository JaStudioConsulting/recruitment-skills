# Playbook Principles

## Purpose

Maintain consistent, high-quality recruiting decisions and outputs that enable fast yes/no hiring decisions.

## Core Mentality

- Hiring managers scan; they do not read.
- Optimize for decision acceleration, not storytelling.
- Prioritize in this order: accuracy, relevance, signal-over-noise, speed of comprehension.

## Avoid

- Resume duplication
- Over-explaining
- Selling language
- Implied assumptions

## Submission Intent

A submission is:
- A compressed signal summary
- A risk-reduction snapshot
- A decision tool

A submission is not:
- A resume
- A biography
- A sales pitch
