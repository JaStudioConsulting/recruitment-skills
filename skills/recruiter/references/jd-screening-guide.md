# JD Screening Guide

## Build Pain Buckets

Extract four role pain buckets before writing questions:
1. Environment: volume, process type, pace, constraints
2. Scope: line support, launches, planning, quality systems
3. Systems/tools: ERP/MRP, KPI dashboards, PFMEA/APQP, SCADA/PLC, or role-relevant stack
4. Risk/retention: commute, shift, travel, eligibility, timeline, stability

## Build a 6-8 Question Set

Use this distribution:
- Must-have proof: 3 questions
- Environment proof: 2 questions
- Risk probe: 1-2 questions
- Logistics confirmation: 1 question

## Canonical Question Bank

Must-have proof:
- Walk through the last improvement you owned end-to-end. What changed and why?
- Which KPI(s) did you own, how did you track them, and what actions did you take when they drifted?
- Describe a launch or major change. What risks did you identify and how did you control timing?

Environment proof:
- What production or operating environment are you used to (volume, takt, variability)? What failed most often?
- Which systems/tools were used daily (ERP/MRP, downtime tracking, quality tools)?

Risk probes:
- Why are you leaving specifically, and what would have made you stay?
- Describe a disagreement with production/quality/stakeholders. What decision did you make?

Logistics:
- Which location/shift works, and what is a realistic start timeline?

## Answer Grading

- Green: specific ownership, concrete example, measurable or scoped result, constraints handled
- Yellow: general experience, shared ownership, light detail
- Red: vague answers, no ownership, blame-shifting, avoids constraints/compliance

## Red Flags

- Frequent "we" statements without personal decision ownership
- Blaming other teams without corrective action
- Avoiding safety/compliance or documentation requirements
- Unclear timelines, certifications, or logistics for role feasibility
