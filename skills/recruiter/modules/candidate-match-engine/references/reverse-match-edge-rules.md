# Reverse-match edge rules

- Derive industry from resume evidence. A matching title alone is insufficient; flag inferred industry.
- Distinguish salary floor from target. Curated results should reach the stated target unless Ja asks for floor-only results.
- Exclude staffing agencies for direct-employer lists. Do not treat an agency posting as a client opportunity.
- Match level and ownership, not title similarity. Never promote exposure to expertise or employer capability to individual capability.
- Prefer two independently verified decision-maker contacts per qualified company when requested. Report a shortfall rather than inventing a contact.
- Verify current posting status and direct official links; mark stale or conflicting evidence.
