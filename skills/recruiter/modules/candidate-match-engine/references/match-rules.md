# Match Rules

This repository stores matching method, not live candidates, client contacts, compensation, submissions, or active job orders.

## Authority order

1. Current approved job description or recruiter intake
2. Candidate resume
3. Candidate transcript or call notes
4. Current submission ledger and Do Not Submit evidence
5. Ja-direct clarification

Old role examples, cached candidate lists, and remembered client requirements are never authority.

## Hard-gate method

For each current role, extract a fresh checklist:

- mandatory licence, certification, education, or work status
- required equipment, software, process, industry, or scope
- location, commute, travel, shift, and onsite requirements
- minimum experience and leadership level
- compensation only when explicitly supplied
- duplicate-submission and Do Not Submit status

Classify each requirement as Confirmed, Not Confirmed, or Conflicting. Not Confirmed means NEEDS VERIFICATION, never Qualified. A failed non-negotiable means NO-GO.

## Nice-to-have method

Score nice-to-haves only after every hard gate passes. Never use a broad title match to override a missing licence, location conflict, shift conflict, duplicate submission, or unsupported skill.

## Privacy boundary

Do not add candidate names, client contacts, live job details, email addresses, compensation records, or submission history to this file. Keep operational facts in approved live systems.
