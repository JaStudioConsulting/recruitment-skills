# Recruiting HR Collection

Internal `$recruiter` collection for compensation analysis, offer drafting, interview preparation, onboarding, organization planning, people reports, performance reviews, policy lookup, and recruiting-pipeline support.

The interview-prep child creates interviewer questions, panel plans, and scorecards. A request for "interview prep material" means the candidate-facing company and role PDF owned by `modules/interview-prep-material/GUIDE.md`, not this collection.

Route through `skills/recruiter/SKILL.md` first. Then read only the matching child `GUIDE.md` in this directory. Child guides are internal modules, not standalone recruiting front doors.

No external send, employee-record write, offer issuance, policy approval, or hiring decision occurs without Ja's explicit authorization.
