# Candidate prospecting and campaign learning

Candidate prospecting markets a verified candidate for current or future consideration. It does not prove a company is hiring, interested, or permitted to receive a profile.

- Build candidate truth from resume, transcript/notes, and approved records. Never infer achievements, interest, availability, compensation, or target-company preference.
- Inspect Gmail Sent mail read-only before learning or drafting. Confirm mailbox, bound dates, count unique message and thread IDs, and sample companies, decision-maker layers, dates, and initial/follow-up variants.
- One sent message is an example, not a campaign. Three separate messages under one subject establish a repeated pattern; five or more across two companies support a stable strategy observation. Sent state does not prove opens, replies, or interest.
- Strip signatures, disclaimers, tracking links, quoted chains, recipient addresses, and secrets from durable learning.
- Use operating proof, genuine company relevance, no vacancy assumption, and one permission-based CTA. Follow-ups add context, not empty reminders. Planning or learning never authorizes a send, Loxo write, or Tracker write.
