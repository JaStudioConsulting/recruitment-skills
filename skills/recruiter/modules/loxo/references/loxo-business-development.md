## BD Operating Model

Loxo BD connects:

1. Sales pipeline/deals
2. Company/account sourcing
3. Decision-maker discovery
4. MPC/spec CV candidate pitching
5. Outreach campaigns
6. Deal tracking and activities

Use this when Ja is marketing a candidate to target companies or building client-side business development motion.

## Account-Based Prospecting

Known flow:

1. Go to Companies.
2. Add Company.
3. Select Loxo Source when available.
4. Search/filter target companies.
5. Open company or select companies.
6. Find decision-makers or view employees.
7. Filter employees by title, location, or seniority.
8. Fetch contact details where available.
9. Add people to list, campaign, or job pipeline.

Notes:

- Loxo Source access may be plan-gated.
- Bulk selection was noted as up to 25 people in gathered research.
- Lists can often be created on the fly; jobs/campaigns should exist before adding people to them.

## Source Leads In Companies

Use Companies as the account-management surface.

Known company record areas:

- Overview
- Contacts
- Employees
- Website and social URLs
- Company status
- Pinned contacts
- Deals

Known operations:

- Search companies with Boolean-enabled search or filters.
- Open Contacts tab to see existing contacts already in the database.
- Open Employees tab to discover possible decision-makers when plan permits.
- Pin key contacts to the company overview.
- Edit company info.
- Set company status.
- Create a deal from company overview.

## MPC / Spec CV

Use for proactive candidate marketing.

Known flow from candidate profile:

1. Open the candidate profile.
2. Use Pitch Candidate or Share, depending on whether candidate is already in a job.
3. Configure what candidate details are shared.
4. Continue.
5. Choose digital link or PDF attachment.
6. Send or prepare the pitch.

Known flow from People pipeline/grid:

1. Select candidate(s).
2. Choose Pitch.
3. Review what profile details are included.

Notes:

- Agency branding/logo can appear on branded profiles/resumes.
- Pitched candidates may be tracked in a Pitch pipeline/tab.
- Always review included profile data before sharing externally.

## Deals And Sales Pipelines

Deals are the BD opportunity surface.

Pipeline setup:

1. Go to Sales CRM/Deals.
2. Create a new pipeline.
3. Name pipeline uniquely.
4. Add stages.
5. Assign win probability by stage.
6. Add rotting age/task aging where useful.
7. Create.

Use deal stages for process movement and deal status for outcome:

- In Progress
- Won
- Lost

Prior notes warn that deals may not move between pipelines after creation; verify before building a pipeline.

## Create Deal

Known creation routes:

- Deals page
- Contact profile
- Company page

Recommended fields:

- Deal name
- Organization/company
- Point of contact
- Amount/value if known
- Owner
- Expected close date
- Pipeline
- Starting stage

Point of contact should be linked to the company record.

## Deal Cards And Activities

Deal cards centralize BD work.

Known activity types:

- Note
- Email
- Meeting
- Call
- Task

Known add activity flow:

1. Open deal card.
2. Use Add activity.
3. Select activity type.
4. Enter details.
5. Save.

Known task flow:

1. Open deal card.
2. New task.
3. Add status, notes, due date, linked records.
4. Save.

Known document flow:

1. Open deal card.
2. Documents section.
3. Add/upload file.

Use this for client conversations, candidate pitch follow-up, agreements, and next steps.

## Merge Deals

Use only for duplicates or highly overlapping deals.

Known rules:

- Open deal card.
- Use the menu/three dots.
- Select Merge.
- Add deal(s) to merge.
- Choose fields to keep.
- Combine multi-value fields such as notes, tasks, events, smart tags where appropriate.
- Merge.

Research says deals must be in the same pipeline to merge.

## Outreach For BD

Known routes:

- From company pinned contacts.
- From Employees tab.
- From deal card contact details.

Important caution:

- Outreach from a deal may contact only the point of contact, not every linked person. Verify before sending.

Use campaigns for multi-contact BD outreach and deal activities for one-to-one logged communication.

## Candidate Marketing Pattern

For a synthetic candidate-to-target-account example:

1. Add or verify target-account company records.
2. Add contacts as Contacts, not Candidates.
3. Tag with task-approved neutral campaign labels such as `Quality Hiring` and `MPC`.
