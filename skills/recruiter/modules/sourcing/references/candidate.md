# Candidate Mode

Use this reference for candidate discovery, supplied-list audits, and full candidate runs.

## Required Target

Build a compact target card from available material:

- Role and hiring client
- Client parent, divisions, and known subsidiaries
- Location and realistic commute/relocation boundary
- Compensation range when known
- Required industry or defensible adjacent industries
- Must-have skills, licenses, certifications, systems, and environment
- Seniority floor and ceiling
- Explicit exclusions

A full JD is helpful, not mandatory. Proceed from a sufficiently specific role brief. Ask for
the hiring client when it is missing because client-employment conflict cannot be checked
without it.

## Hard Gates Before Fit

Assign `Excluded` before scoring when public or connected-system evidence confirms:

- Current employment by the hiring client, its parent, division, or subsidiary
- Do Not Contact or Do Not Submit status
- Ownership conflict that Ja cannot act on
- Prior submission to the same client/role requiring duplicate review

Current employment by a supplier, customer, or competitor is not an affiliate conflict unless
official evidence shows common corporate ownership.

Previous client employment is not an automatic exclusion. Flag it for review.

## Discovery

Create up to five distinct searches using:

1. Direct and alternate titles
2. Must-have skill or certification
3. Target and adjacent industries
4. Location or commute region
5. Public-profile operator such as `site:linkedin.com/in`

Vary title coverage rather than producing near-duplicate strings. Check the client corporate
family before recommending anyone.

## Candidate Ratings

- `Strong`: Direct industry/scope match, core requirements publicly supported, plausible
  logistics, no hard-gate conflict.
- `Possible`: Credible match with one material item to confirm.
- `Stretch`: Several important gaps, adjacent background, level mismatch, or weak public proof.
- `Reject`: Clear technical, industry, seniority, or logistics mismatch.
- `Excluded`: Hard conflict. Do not rank above eligible candidates.

Confidence measures evidence quality, not enthusiasm:

- `High`: Current identity and core decision evidence independently supported.
- `Medium`: Identity is supported but title, scope, or a key requirement is hidden.
- `Low`: Supplied claim cannot be independently verified or sources conflict.

## Candidate Table

Use exactly:

| Rank | Name | Verified Role/Location | Status | Fit | Evidence | Gaps | Public Profile | Confidence |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- `Status`: Eligible, Excluded, Outdated, Conflicting, or Unconfirmed.
- Rank eligible people first. Put excluded and rejected rows after eligible rows while preserving
  one row per unique person.
- Cover every unique supplied candidate exactly once.

After the table provide:

**Excluded:** names plus one-line conflict reason.

**Top three:** names in contact order plus one-line reason.

**Confirm before contact:** only questions that could change eligibility or fit, such as
commute, compensation, work status, certification, program ownership, availability, or
interest.
