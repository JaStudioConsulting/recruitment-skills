# Prospect Mode

Use this reference for business-development contact discovery, supplied prospect audits, and
full prospect runs.

## Required Target

Build a compact ideal-prospect card:

- Service or staffing offer
- Target account, company list, or company profile
- Industry and geography
- Decision-maker and influencer titles
- Hiring, growth, operational, leadership, or compliance signals
- Existing-client, ownership, DNC, or account exclusions
- Requested count

If the requested offer or decision-maker function is unclear, ask one concise question. Do not
run a broad people search with no commercial target.

## Prospect Conflict Logic

Target-account employees are desired. Do not exclude them because they work for the company
being researched.

Assign `Excluded` when evidence confirms:

- Do Not Contact status
- Account or contact ownership conflict
- Existing-client restriction supplied by Ja
- Duplicate contact already active in the same campaign/list
- Person is outside the requested function, geography, or company set and has no defensible
  influence on the buying decision

## Discovery

Use up to five distinct searches covering:

1. Economic buyer titles
2. Operational decision-maker titles
3. HR/talent or functional influencer titles
4. Company and geography
5. Current hiring, expansion, leadership, compliance, or operational signals

Prefer current company pages, leadership pages, job postings, press releases, public profiles,
and reputable industry sources. Do not invent that a company has a hiring need because an
employee has a relevant title.

## Prospect Ratings

- `High`: Correct account and geography, clear decision-maker/influencer role, plus a current
  business or hiring signal.
- `Medium`: Relevant role and account, but buying influence or current signal needs confirmation.
- `Low`: Adjacent role, weak signal, uncertain authority, or incomplete public evidence.
- `Reject`: Wrong function, account, geography, or clearly irrelevant role.
- `Excluded`: DNC, ownership, duplicate, or explicit account restriction.

Use `High`, `Medium`, or `Low` confidence with the same source-quality meaning as candidate
mode. Do not confuse priority with confidence.

## Prospect Table

Use exactly:

| Priority | Name | Role/Company | Business Signal | Why Target | Risks | Public Profile | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Cover every unique supplied prospect exactly once in the table. Keep rejected and excluded
people at the bottom rather than moving them into prose only.

After the table provide:

**Excluded:** names plus one-line reason.

**Top three:** names in contact order plus the commercial angle supported by evidence.

**Confirm before outreach:** only unresolved issues that could change priority, ownership,
DNC status, authority, or account relevance.
